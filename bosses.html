BOSS GUIDE

Complete boss strategies with attack patterns and weaknesses.

PRISON WARDEN

Area 1 - Tutorial Boss

HP250
Easy

Attack Patterns

Slash Combo

3-hit melee combo. Dodge backward twice.

Guard Charge

Shield bash followed by charge. Wait and punish.

Strategy

Tutorial boss. Focus on dodging, not attacking. Wait for openings. Use 2-3 hits then retreat. Scan for lore entry.

THE BRÙM娃娃

Area 2 - Hive Mini-Boss

HP1,200
Normal

Attack Patterns

Buzz Dash

Horizontal dash attack. Jump over or duck under.

Stinger Rain

Summons falling stingers. Stay mobile.

Phase 2 - Enrage

Faster attacks, spawns worker bees.

Strategy

• Use Wasp Form for aerial advantage

• Focus damage during Buzz Dash recovery

• Phase 2: Clear bees before resuming damage

• Watch for ground telegraph before Stinger Rain

LÙCIA, THE SILK WEAVING GODDESS

Area 4 - Final Boss

HP4,500
Extreme

Attack Patterns

Thread Storm

Sweeping thread attack. Dash through gaps.

Silk Binding

Traps Hornet in silk. Break free quickly.

Clone Split

Creates silk clones. Use sound to identify real.

Phase 2 - Ascension

Flies higher, new silk weave patterns.

Strategy

• Master Thread Lark for aerial combat

• Watch for Clone Split - listen for sound cues

• Phase 2: Stay grounded during Thread Storm

• Use all silk abilities - cycle through them

• Final phase: Patience over aggression

Recommended: All silk abilities unlocked, 10+ charm notches, full health
