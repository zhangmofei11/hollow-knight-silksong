TIPS & TRICKS

Advanced strategies and hidden secrets.

B BEGINNER TIPS

1

Map Every Wall

Stand near walls to reveal map details. The more you explore, the more complete your map becomes.

2

Prioritize Health

Find Health Fragments early. Boss fights are brutal with low health. Always have backup.

3

Learn Attack Tells

Every enemy has a visual cue before attacking. Watch and learn patterns before engaging.

4

Use the Environment

Spikes, pits, and environmental hazards can damage enemies too. Use them strategically.

A ADVANCED STRATEGIES

Silk Combo Chains

Chain silk abilities for massive damage. Thread Toss → Silk Slash → Silk Storm creates devastating combos.

Boss Weakness Exploit

Each boss has elemental weaknesses. Fire bosses hate silk ice. Ice bosses hate fire silk. Experiment!

Health Management

Heal only when safe. Mid-fight heals invite combos. Wait for clear openings to heal.

Backtrack Rewards

New abilities unlock old areas. Return to Area 1 after getting Wall Jump for secret passages.
