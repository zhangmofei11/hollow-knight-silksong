SILK ABILITIES

All silk techniques and翠丝炉 locations.

⚔️ COMBAT ABILITIES

S

Silk Slash

Default Attack

Basic silk-based melee attack. Hold for charged attack with larger range.

D

Silk Dash

Area 1 - Default

Quick horizontal dash. Invincible during dash frames. Essential for dodging.

T

Thread Toss

Area 1 - Early

Ranged silk projectile. Hold to aim. Can pin enemies to walls.

W

Wasp Form

Area 2 - Hive

Aerial transformation. Fly toward cursor. Extended hitbox on contact.

TL

Thread Lark

Area 3 - Fungal

Vertical silk launch. Hold to climb. Creates silk platforms.

SS

Silk Storm

Area 4 - Peak

Spinning silk attack. Multi-hit. Great for groups and breaking armor.

🧵 MOBILITY ABILITIES

Wall Jump

Default

Wall cling and jump. Essential for vertical navigation.

Cobweb Slide

Area 1

Slide along cobwebs at high speed. Opens shortcuts and secret paths.

Silk Grapple

Area 2

Grapple to silk anchor points. Essential for crossing large gaps.

Spore Float

Area 3

Slow descent in spore clouds. Extends air time significantly.

🔥 翠丝炉 LOCATIONS

What is 翠丝炉?

翠丝炉 (Weaver's Blessing) are special shrines that unlock new silk abilities when activated. Each翠丝炉 contains memories of ancient weavers.

Total: 8 Locations

Scattered across all areas. Some require specific abilities to access. All 8 unlock the "Master Weaver" achievement.
